About TechForge India

TechForge India was built as a demo e-commerce project to practice end-to-end digital marketing and SEO on a realistic gaming-hardware storefront — product pages, category pages, a blog, structured data, and a full purchase funnel from add-to-cart to order confirmation.

What we sell (in the demo)

Processors, graphics cards, gaming laptops, monitors and accessories from well-known brands, with pricing modeled on the Indian market.

Why this project exists

  • Practice writing SEO-friendly titles, meta descriptions and structured data (JSON-LD)
  • Experiment with conversion-rate optimisation: urgency banners, coupons, reviews, trust badges
  • Wire up GA4 / GTM / Meta Pixel event tracking (add_to_cart, purchase, view_item, etc.)
  • Run a mock merchant dashboard to see how analytics and inventory connect
